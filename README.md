## <a name='Howtoopenthehousing'></a>How to open the housing
> [!WARNING]  
> The pump is obviously not made to get repaired. Everything is assembled with a snap system. After disassembling some plastic hooks / noses will be defective. Espaccially at the display.

:warning: Discription of full disassembly.
If you only need to fix a dedicated problem use it as reference.

## <a name='Step1:removepump'></a>Step 1: remove pump
This is the easy part. You have 4 inbus screws to remove. Pull the pump out. Thats it.  
<img src="images/20_unmount.png" style="height:200px">

## <a name='Step2:removepumphousing'></a>Step 2: remove pump housing
Look at the housing. There are 4 slots. If you look closely you can see, that there is a snap system in two of the slots.
The good thing is, that you can use a screwdriver to lever out the latching nose. You have to give force to bend out the housing of the downside part.

## <a name='Step3:removedisplay'></a>Step 3: remove display
The display is hold in place by 4 hooks. Use a screwdriver go under the edge, near the hook and carefully lift off the display. You need some force and the plastic hook will break.

<img src="images/31_dis_1.JPG" style="height:200px">

You can see the damage of the hooks and the edge of the display.

## <a name='Step4:unblocktherotordisassemblethepump'></a>Step 4: unblock the rotor / disassemble the pump
One common error is a blocked rotor. The best way to unblock is to lever carefully between the rotor and the housing or press the rotor on a flat surface. After it's loose it should also rotate again.  
<img src="images/52_unblock.png" style="height:200px">

> [!WARNING]  
> Avoid to try to rotate the rotor with much force or to use a screwdriver to rotate it. The rotor is made of light plastic, so it brakes very easy.

You can lever out the water side part of the pump. The result is shown here.  
<img src="images/53.JPG" style="height:200px">
<img src="images/54.JPG" style="height:200px">
<img src="images/55.JPG" style="height:200px">
<img src="images/56.JPG" style="height:200px">  
You can see, that the rotor is fully separated from the electrical side. The motion is generated only with the help of the magnetic field from the coils to the magnetic rotor.
